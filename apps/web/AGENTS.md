# Outreach

A single operator's outreach book. It keeps track of who you know, what you sent
them, and what came of it, across email and WhatsApp.

## Language

### People and places

**Contact**:
One person, reachable on at least one channel, held for exactly one reason. A
person you approach for both freelance work and a job is two contacts, not one.
_Avoid_: Lead, prospect, recipient

**Category**:
The reason a contact exists — `freelance` or `job`. It is fixed at the moment
you add someone and is the top-level split every list is filtered by.
_Avoid_: Type, kind, segment

**Organization**:
A company or client a contact belongs to. A contact can stand alone without one.
_Avoid_: Account, employer

**Company**:
An organization you might work _at_. The counterpart of a client.

**Client**:
An organization that might hire you _for_ work. The counterpart of a company.

**Tag**:
A free-form label on a contact. Tags are one flat namespace — a tag means the
same thing on a freelance contact as it does on a job contact.
_Avoid_: Label, group, list

### Reaching people

**Channel**:
A way of reaching a contact — email or WhatsApp. Each is independently switched
on or off per contact.
_Avoid_: Medium, platform, provider

**Opt-in**:
Recorded permission to message a contact on WhatsApp. An opt-in is never just a
yes: it carries when it was given and, in your own words, where it came from.
_Avoid_: Consent, permission, subscription

**Document**:
A file you attach to outreach — a CV, a portfolio, a rate card. Documents live
in Cloudinary; the book only points at them.
_Avoid_: File, attachment, asset

**Email draft**:
A reusable, named email — a subject, a body, and the documents that ride along
with it by default. A draft is a template, never a thing that was sent.
_Avoid_: Template, message, boilerplate

### Sending

**Campaign**:
One batch of outreach: a channel, a set of contacts, and the draft to use. A
campaign is assembled first and sent second.
_Avoid_: Blast, batch, run

**Confirmation**:
The deliberate act of approving an assembled campaign. Nothing leaves until a
campaign is confirmed; before that it can be edited or thrown away freely.
_Avoid_: Approval, launch, submit

**Send**:
One message to one contact in one campaign. A send holds the exact text that
went out, and it outlives the draft it came from, the campaign that produced it,
and even the contact leaving your book.
_Avoid_: Message, delivery, email

**Channel settings**:
The credentials and provider quota for a single channel — what lets you send at
all, and how much you have left to send today.
_Avoid_: Config, integration, connection
